Set up Your Profile

Your profile is where you manage your personal information, account preferences, and security settings. Keeping your profile up to date helps ensure a secure account and a personalized experience across the Arivu platform.

Arivu AdminJul 18, 20262 min readAccount Setup and Management

Access Your Profile Settings

  1. Select your Profile icon at the top right corner of the page.

  2. Select Your Profile.

Your Avatar

Your profile photo helps teammates identify you across the platform.

You can:

  • Upload a new profile picture.

  • Replace an existing picture.

  • Remove your current picture.

Supported file types:

  • PNG

  • JPG

  • GIF

  • WEBP

  • SVG

Maximum file size: 10 MB

Tip! A square image provides the best results.

Personal Information

Manage the information that is visible to your teammates.

  1. First Name. Update your first name.

  2. Last Name. Update your last name.

  3. Phone Number. Add or update your phone number.

  4. Username. Your username is read-only and cannot be changed.

  5. Email Address. Your email address is read-only and cannot be changed.

  6. Once done, select Save Changes.

Appearance

Customize how Arivu looks during your sessions.

Available themes include:

  • Light – Bright surfaces with a classic appearance.

  • Dark – Optimized for low-light environments.

  • System – Automatically matches your device's theme settings.

Your appearance preference applies only to your own account.

Sign-In and Security

Keep your account secure by regularly reviewing your security settings.

Change Your Password


To update your password:

  1. Select Change Password.

  2. Enter your Current password.

  3. Create a New password.

  4. Re-enter your New Password.

  5. Once done, select Update Password.

Best Practice. Use a long, unique passphrase that you don't reuse on other websites or applications.

Last Sign-In

Review your most recent login information, including:

  • Date

  • Time

This helps you identify any unexpected account activity.

Account Status

This section displays your current account status, such as:

  • Active

  • Suspended (if applicable)

It may also indicate whether your account is an internal user account.

Workspace & Access

View the applications and workspaces assigned to your account.

For each application, you'll see your assigned role, such as:

  • Admin

  • Auditor

  • Customer

  • Member

This section is informational and reflects the permissions granted by your organization.
