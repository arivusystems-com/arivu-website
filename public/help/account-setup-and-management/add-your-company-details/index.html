Add Your Company Details

Your company details define your organization's identity and the default regional settings used across the Arivu platform. Keeping this information up to date ensures consistent branding, accurate localization, and a unified experience for your users.

Arivu SystemJul 30, 20261 min readAccount Setup and Management

Access Your Company Details

  1. Select your Profile icon at the top right corner of the page.

  2. Select Settings from the drop-down.

  3. Select Company Details.

Branding

Customize your organization's branding to provide a consistent experience across the platform.

You can update the following:

  1. Company Name. Update your company name.

  2. Brand Color. Choose your company's primary brand color. Enter the color using a hexadecimal (Hex) value, for example, #3a1f8a.

  3. Company Logo. Upload or replace your company logo.

    1. Supported file types:

      1. PNG

      2. JPG

      3. SVG

      4. GIF

      5. WEBP

    2. Maximum file size: 10 MB

    3. Recommended image size: 512 × 512 pixels

  4. Once done, select Save Changes.

Tip! A square logo provides the best display quality across the platform.

Regional & Localization

Configure the default regional settings used throughout your organization.


Timezone

Select the default timezone for your organization.

Changing the timezone affects how dates and times are displayed across the platform.

Default Currency

Choose the currency used for monetary values across the platform, including:

  • Deals

  • Invoices

  • Reports

Language

Select the default language for new users joining your organization.

Existing users can choose their own preferred language from their personal settings.

Locale

Select the locale used for formatting:

  • Dates

  • Numbers

  • Currency

Locales follow the format language-COUNTRY, for example:

  • en-US

  • en-IN

  • fr-FR

Data Region

The Data Region indicates where your organization's data is stored.

This value is assigned during onboarding and is read-only.

It cannot be changed after your organization has been created.
