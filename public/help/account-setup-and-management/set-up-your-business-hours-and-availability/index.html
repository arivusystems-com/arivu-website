Set Up Your Business Hours and Availability

Arivu SystemJul 18, 20262 min readAccount Setup and Management

Access Business Hours & Availability

  1. Select your Profile icon at the top right corner of the page.

  2. Select Settings from the drop-down.

  3. Select Business Hours & Availability.

Schedules

The Schedules section allows you to create and manage working hour schedules for your company, teams, or individual users.


Each schedule displays:

  • Schedule name.

  • Scope.

  • Current status.

  • Working days and hours.

  • Assigned timezone.

Company schedules are used as the default availability across the organization, while individual schedules can be used to override company hours where required.

Create a Schedule

To create a new schedule:

  1. Select Create Schedule.

  2. Enter a Name for the schedule.

  3. Select the appropriate Scope.

  4. Choose whether the schedule is Active.

  5. Select the Timezone.

  6. Optionally assign a Holiday Calendar.

  7. Choose whether to Allow scheduling outside these hours (with confirmation).

  8. Configure the weekly schedule.

  9. For each day of the week, you can:

  • Set working hours.

  • Mark the day as Closed.

  • Add one or more breaks.

  • Copy Monday's schedule to the remaining weekdays.

  1. Once done, select Create Schedule.

Tip! Create separate schedules for different teams or regions if your organization operates across multiple time zones.

Your Availability

The My Availability section displays the schedule currently assigned to your account.

If your organization uses a company default schedule, it will be displayed here.

Personal schedule overrides are managed by an administrator and cannot be modified from this page.

Holiday Calendars

Holiday calendars allow you to exclude public holidays and company holidays from working schedules.

When a holiday calendar is linked to a schedule:

  • Bookings skip holidays.

  • SLA timers exclude holidays.

  • Availability reflects non-working days.

You can import holiday calendars using a CSV file.

Best Practice. Keep your holiday calendars updated each year to ensure bookings and SLA calculations remain accurate.

Operational Insights

The Operational Insights section provides schedule-based metrics to help you understand how your organization is performing during and outside business hours.

Available insights include:

  • Capacity for the selected period.

  • Scheduled open minutes.

  • In-hours activity.

  • Overtime activity.

  • Off-hours SLA breaches.

  • Deferred assignments and automations.

You can:

  • Select a date range.

  • Refresh the report.

  • Recompute the previous day's metrics.

The Daily Trend section provides a day-by-day breakdown of:

  • In-hours activity.

  • Overtime activity.

  • Off-hours SLA breaches.

These insights help identify scheduling gaps, monitor workload outside business hours, and optimize staffing across your organization.
